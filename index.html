01 — HERITAGE

Historical Places & Monuments

Every stone here was placed on purpose. Tap "View location" to see exactly where each monument stands today.

▲
Old Kingdom · c. 2560 BC

The Great Pyramid of Giza

Built for Pharaoh Khufu (Cheops)

The last surviving wonder of the ancient world, once the tallest man-made structure on Earth for nearly 3,800 years. It was built as an eternal home for Khufu's journey into the afterlife.

📍 View location
🦁
Old Kingdom · c. 2500 BC

The Great Sphinx

Attributed to Pharaoh Khafre

A limestone guardian with a lion's body and a pharaoh's face, carved from a single ridge of bedrock to watch over the Giza plateau.

📍 View location
🏛️
New Kingdom · c. 1970 BC – 100 AD

Karnak Temple Complex

Expanded by over 30 pharaohs, including Thutmose III & Ramesses II

The largest religious building ever constructed, built and rebuilt across two thousand years of continuous worship of the god Amun.

📍 View location
🏛️
New Kingdom · c. 1400 BC

Luxor Temple

Built chiefly by Amenhotep III, expanded by Ramesses II

Unlike most temples, Luxor was not dedicated to a god but to kingship itself — it's where many pharaohs may have been crowned, and it once hosted the great Opet Festival procession from Karnak.

📍 View location
🗿
New Kingdom · c. 1264 BC

Abu Simbel

Built for Pharaoh Ramesses II

Twin rock-cut temples carved into a mountainside, so precisely aligned that sunlight reaches the inner sanctuary only twice a year. The entire site was relocated in the 1960s to save it from flooding.

📍 View location
🏛️
Ptolemaic Period · c. 380 BC

Temple of Philae, Aswan

Dedicated to the goddess Isis

An island sanctuary devoted to Isis, moved stone by stone to Agilkia Island near Aswan as part of a landmark UNESCO rescue campaign in the 1970s.

📍 View location
▲
Old Kingdom · c. 2670 BC

Step Pyramid of Saqqara

Designed by the architect Imhotep for Pharaoh Djoser

The oldest large-scale cut-stone building in the world, and the very first pyramid ever built — the prototype every later pyramid at Giza descended from.

📍 View location
𓂀
New Kingdom · c. 1550–1069 BC

Valley of the Kings

Royal necropolis of the New Kingdom pharaohs

A hidden desert valley where pharaohs — including Tutankhamun, Seti I, and Ramesses the Great — were buried in deep rock-cut tombs to protect them from robbers.

📍 View location
02 — LEGACY

Great Egyptian People

Egypt's story didn't end in antiquity. These are some of the people carrying it forward.

❤️

Sir Magdi Yacoub

Pioneering heart transplant surgeon

One of the world's foremost cardiothoracic surgeons, credited with performing more heart transplants than anyone in history and founding charities that fund life-saving heart surgery for children.

⚽

Mohamed Salah

Footballer, Liverpool FC & Egypt national team

Egypt's most celebrated modern athlete, known worldwide for his goal-scoring at Liverpool FC and for the youth academy he built in his hometown of Nagrig.

⚛️

Samira Moussa

Nuclear physicist

A brilliant nuclear scientist who worked toward making atomic energy affordable and medically accessible, earning her the nickname "Mother of Atomic Energy" in Egypt.

Shajar al-Durr

Sultana of Egypt, 13th century

One of the only women to ever rule Egypt in her own name, she led the defense of the country during the Seventh Crusade and founded the Mamluk era. No verified portrait of her survives, so she's shown here as an illustrated 2D character.

📖

Taha Hussein

Writer & reformer, "Dean of Arabic Literature"

Blind from early childhood, he became one of the most influential intellectuals in the modern Arab world and reshaped Egyptian education. His autobiography, Al-Ayyam (The Days), remains a landmark of Arabic literature.

🖋️

Naguib Mahfouz

Novelist, Nobel laureate in Literature (1988)

The first Arabic-language writer to win the Nobel Prize in Literature, best known for the Cairo Trilogy, which follows a Cairo family across three generations.

🔬

Ahmed Zewail

Chemist, Nobel laureate in Chemistry (1999)

Known as the "father of femtochemistry," he won the Nobel Prize for developing techniques to observe chemical reactions on a femtosecond timescale — fast enough to watch atoms move.

🎬

Omar Sharif

Actor

Egypt's first true international film star, best known for Lawrence of Arabia and Doctor Zhivago, and later celebrated as a world-class bridge player.

🎙️

Umm Kulthum

Singer, "Star of the East"

The most celebrated singer in the history of Arabic music, whose monthly live radio concerts could empty the streets of Cairo for hours at a time.

03 — CHRONOLOGY

A Timeline of Egyptian History

Ordered by time, because that's the one thing that actually matters here.

c. 6000 BC

Predynastic Period

Farming communities settle along the Nile, laying the groundwork for one of the first great civilizations.

c. 3100 BC

Unification of Egypt

King Narmer unites Upper and Lower Egypt, founding the first dynasty and the world's first nation-state.

2686–2181 BC

The Old Kingdom

The "Age of the Pyramids" — Giza, Saqqara, and the great age of monumental stone building.

2055–1650 BC

The Middle Kingdom

A golden age of literature, art, and stability after a period of division.

1550–1069 BC

The New Kingdom

Egypt's imperial peak — Tutankhamun, Ramesses II, and the great temples of Luxor and Karnak.

332 BC

Ptolemaic Egypt

Alexander the Great founds Alexandria; the Ptolemaic dynasty rules until Cleopatra VII.

30 BC – 641 AD

Roman & Byzantine Egypt

Egypt becomes the breadbasket of Rome, and later a center of early Christianity.

641 AD

Islamic Egypt

The Arab conquest begins a new era, founding Cairo (Al-Qahira) as a center of learning and trade.

1952

The Modern Republic

The 1952 revolution ends the monarchy, and Egypt steps into its modern era.

05 — MYTHOLOGY

Ancient Gods & Symbols

The forces the ancient Egyptians believed shaped life, death, and everything after.

☀️

Ra

God of the Sun

The king of the gods, sailing across the sky by day and through the underworld by night, reborn each dawn.

🐺

Anubis

God of Mummification & the Afterlife

Jackal-headed guardian of the dead, who weighed the hearts of souls against the feather of truth.

🪶

Isis

Goddess of Magic & Motherhood

Devoted wife of Osiris and mother of Horus, revered as the ultimate protector and healer.

🌾

Osiris

God of the Afterlife & Rebirth

Ruler of the underworld, whose death and resurrection myth symbolized the yearly flooding and rebirth of the Nile.

🦅

Horus

God of the Sky & Kingship

The falcon-headed god whose eyes were the sun and moon — every pharaoh was considered his living embodiment.

📜

Thoth

God of Wisdom & Writing

Ibis-headed inventor of writing and keeper of divine knowledge, credited with recording the judgment of every soul.

🦁

Sekhmet

Goddess of War & Healing

The lioness warrior goddess who could bring plague or cure it — feared and petitioned in equal measure.

🐈

Bastet

Goddess of Home & Protection

The lioness-turned-cat goddess who guarded the household and warded off evil spirits.
